# Recent Product and Marketing Innovations in Frozen Yogurt / Frozen Dessert (US, 2024-2026)

Scope note: Per assignment, Menchie's, Pinkberry, sweetFrog, TCBY, 16 Handles, Red Mango, and Yochi/Yo-Chi are excluded from "new concept" findings except as incidental context. Note: a brand called **Yo-Chi** (Australian, opened its first US store Oct 3, 2026 in Dallas) is a different/same-named entity from the "Yochi" on the excluded list — included here only as brief context since it is explicitly on the exclusion list and is treated as already-known.

## What new frozen yogurt or soft-serve brands/concepts have launched or gained attention recently (2024-2026), excluding the 7 named brands?

### Takeaway
The most notable new-concept activity is a "third wave" of **Greek-style, high-protein, clean-label froyo** — small, founder-driven shops and pop-ups (Mikono, Kouklas, Ema/Rotisserie Ema in Chicago; Myka/MYKA Greek Frozen Yogurt out of Madrid; Stelly's Froyo in New Orleans; Fira and Birdie's in Brooklyn) that position froyo as a premium, protein-forward, Instagrammable product distinct from 2010s self-serve chains. Simultaneously, legacy ice cream brands (Van Leeuwen) and CPG/DTC protein brands (Yasso/Magnum, Protein Pints, David Protein) are entering frozen yogurt/high-protein frozen dessert from the premium and grocery sides, respectively, partly in response to GLP-1-driven changes in frozen-treat consumption.

Its first U.S. location opened December 8, 2025 at Robert Rivani's Lincoln Theatre in South Beach, Miami — an Art Deco landmark venue. Product: Greek yogurt imported weekly from Greece blended with pasteurized milk and "high-end kefir" to preserve probiotics/protein/texture without artificial additives; in-store workshop model with 30+ gourmet toppings, rotating seasonal flavors, vegan/lactose-free sorbets. Plans to expand into more U.S. and Latin American markets during 2026. — [Its Just Business Magazine](https://www.itsjustbusinessmagazine.com/myka-brings-cult-favorite-greek-yogurt-ice-cream-to-the-u-s-with-first-location-at-robert-rivanis-iconic-lincoln-theatre/); [National Herald on NY debut](https://www.thenationalherald.com/start-spreading-the-news-myka-greek-frozen-yogurt-is-making-its-new-york-debut/); [Revista Hosteleria — 19 countries](https://www.revistahosteleria.com/texto-diario/mostrar/5856599/myka-supera-19-paises)

Twelve new froyo food trucks/trailers launched globally in 2025, 8 of them in the U.S. — [IFYA Froyo Year in Review 2025](https://internationalfrozenyogurt.com/news/the-froyo-year-in-review-2025-edition/)

### Inferences
- The center of gravity for "new and notable" in U.S. froyo has shifted from large self-serve chains (2000s-2010s model) toward two poles: (1) small, founder-led, Greek-yogurt-based, high-protein, "clean label" independent shops/pop-ups positioning froyo as a premium/health product, often explicitly citing Mediterranean-brand inspiration (Myka/Mikono/Fira), and (2) established premium food brands (Van Leeuwen) adding froyo as a lower-calorie extension to defend against GLP-1-driven volume declines in traditional ice cream.
- The "Greek froyo" wave reads as directly adjacent to, but distinct from, the Greek-yogurt CPG boom (Chobani, Fage) — it's applying that positioning to the frozen/soft-serve format specifically.

## What notable flavor or ingredient innovations have appeared across the category recently?

### Takeaway
2025-2026 flavor innovation leans into three threads: (1) high-protein/functional reformulation (pint-sized spoonable frozen Greek yogurt, cottage-cheese froyo), (2) music/entertainment IP collaborations with limited-time flavors tied to specific artists or films, and (3) adventurous sweet-savory flavor mashups (fig & feta, honey cornbread, moutai spirit) at independent shops. The International Frozen Yogurt Association (IFYA) tracked 88 new froyo/alternative flavors introduced globally in 2025 (vs. 53 in 2024), with MYKA Greek alone launching 24 new flavors.

### Cited Findings
- **Yasso** (owned by The Magnum Ice Cream Company, spun off from Unilever) launched its first-ever pint-sized frozen Greek yogurt line, exclusive to Target starting October 12, 2025, with national rollout the following year. Five flavors: Mint Chocolate Chip, Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough, Fudge Brownie, Cookies 'n Cream, Coffee Chip; up to 19g protein per serving, ~400 calories per 14-oz pint, no artificial colors/flavors/sugar alcohols. CMO Lisa Vortsman called it "a pivotal moment" for the brand. — [Food Dive](https://www.fooddive.com/news/magnum-taps-into-better-for-you-craze-with-yasso-frozen-greek-yogurt-pints/802352/); [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/yasso-reimagines-better-for-you-ice-cream-with-protein-packed-spoonable-frozen-greek-yogurt-302572246.html); [Food Business News](https://www.foodbusinessnews.net/articles/29175-yasso-formulates-its-first-pint-sized-frozen-greek-yogurt)
- **JVKE x Yogurtland**: Limited-time "Red Velvet" flavor ("this is what red velvet tastes like") ran September 1–October 19, 2025, served in a branded red cup with an exclusive branded spoon, topped with JVKE-branded "Sound Bites" (pink & green music-note gummies) and "Beat Drops" (mini chocolate chips) — an artist-flavor-merch tie-in. — [QSR Magazine](https://www.qsrmagazine.com/news/jvke-collaboration-brings-music-themed-frozen-yogurt-to-yogurtland/); [Yahoo Finance/PR](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/yogurtland-singer-songwriter-record-producer-170300373.html)

### Inferences
- High-protein/"better-for-you" reformulation (spoonable pints, 18-30g protein, low sugar) is now the dominant axis of flavor/product innovation in frozen yogurt-adjacent frozen desserts, spanning both legacy yogurt brands (Yasso) and new DTC protein brands (Protein Pints, David Protein) — suggesting the innovation frontier has partly migrated from flavor novelty toward macro-nutrient engineering.
- IP/artist/film collaborations (JVKE, Back to the Future, HI-CHEW) function as a recurring limited-time-offer (LTO) marketing lever specifically for the larger chains, generating earned media and merch-like branded serveware alongside the flavor itself.

## Are there notable marketing campaign formats being used that stand out as innovative?

### Takeaway
Beyond flavor LTOs, the standout 2025-2026 marketing formats are: branded-content/artist collaborations with exclusive merch-like serveware (Yogurtland x JVKE), day-based social activations (National Dog Day, Giant Spoon Day), micro-influencer-driven UGC strategies measured by a formal industry ranking (IFYA's social media leaderboard), and price-stunt grand-opening promotions ($1 froyo days) used by both legacy and new entrants. Traditional cause-marketing/fundraising programs (percentage-back nights) remain a baseline tactic industry-wide rather than a point of innovation.

### Cited Findings
- **Yogurtland's broader marketing playbook** (under marketing lead Brittany Knollmiller, per QSR Magazine) combines celebrity/IP collaborations — including with **PAC-MAN** and GRAMMY-winning artist **Laufey** — with recurring social-activation "moments" like Back to School, National Dog Day, and "Holiday Giant Spoon Day," blending macro- and micro-influencer amplification. — [QSR Magazine](https://www.qsrmagazine.com/story/how-brittany-knollmiller-is-redefining-yogurtlands-marketing-playbook/) (full article behind a 403 paywall for direct fetch; details drawn from search-engine summary of the piece)
- **IFYA's "Frozen Yogurt Brands Winning Social Media in 2026"** formally ranks 15 global froyo brands on audience size, engagement rate, content velocity/consistency, and cultural relevance/UGC. **Yo-Chi** (Australia) ranked #1 across Instagram and TikTok, ahead of Yogurtland and Menchie's (both U.S.); Philippines-based **BLK 513** ranked 5th for "trend-driven, high TikTok virality, strong youth engagement." Notably, **The Bigg Chill**, a single-location LA shop, was called out for outperforming larger chains (37.4K Instagram followers, individual posts drawing 789 shares) purely through daily short-form video, staff/behind-the-scenes storytelling, and resharing user-generated content — illustrating that a small independent can out-market big chains on social. — [IFYA](https://internationalfrozenyogurt.com/news/the-frozen-yogurt-brands-winning-social-media-in-2026/)
- **Van Leeuwen** used "$1 small froyo" launch-day promotions repeatedly as it rolled its new frozen yogurt line market-by-market in 2026 (Miami/Brickell on April 9, Washington D.C. in July, Boston in September) — a low-cost trial/sampling mechanic paired with local press/event listings to drive grand-opening foot traffic city by city. — [Community Newspapers](https://communitynewspapers.com/event/1-small-frozen-yogurt-at-van-leeuwen-brickell-to-celebrate-new-launch-on-april-9th/); [PoPville](https://www.popville.com/2026/07/one-dollar-fro-yo-washington-dc-van-leeuwen/); [Boston Calendar](https://www.thebostoncalendar.com/events/1-fro-yo-for-van-leeuwen-fro-yo-launch)
- Cause-marketing/fundraising nights remain a standard (not newly innovative) tactic: Yogurtini offers groups 20% of sales back for a cause; Yogurtland offers 15-20% back for group fundraisers; historically, Orange Leaf ran a 300+ location campaign that raised $132,911 (against a $100,000 goal) to fund meals for hungry children via the Booster t-shirt-fundraising platform. IFYA publishes an annual "Froyo Gives Back" roundup (2025 edition referenced but not independently viewed). — [GroupRaise - Yogurtini](https://www.groupraise.com/yogurtini-frozen-yogurt/locations/34439); [GroupRaise on Yogurtland](https://www.groupraise.com/articles/how-yogurtland-collaborates-with-nonprofit-organizations-for-fundraising-initiatives); [HuffPost on Orange Leaf](https://www.huffpost.com/entry/wow-this-company-turned-f_b_8625178)
- Loyalty-program activity is present but appears incremental rather than innovative: Yogurtland's "Real Rewards" app uses a conventional points system (2 points/$1, 100 points = $5 reward) with three tiers (Signature/Premium/Ultimate) and birthday/bonus-point perks; TCBY's app uses a similar $1 = 10 points model; a dedicated "YoFroYo Loyalty" app released in 2025. No brand was found running dedicated mini-game or challenge-based gamification mechanics as of this research. — [Yogurtland Real Rewards](https://www.yogurtland.com/realrewards); [TCBY app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tcby-frozen-yogurt/id1222063860); [YoFroYo Loyalty app](https://apps.apple.com/pk/app/yofroyo-loyalty/id6758517704)
- Athlete/celebrity-partnership marketing cited in adjacent (non-frozen, spoonable) yogurt category for comparison/context: Oikos' "Surprising Strength" campaign paired actress Juno Temple with NFL player Myles Garrett; Noosa Yoghurt partnered with Peloton instructor Ally Love. These are spoonable-yogurt (not frozen) campaigns but indicate the broader yogurt category's playbook of pairing comedic/lifestyle talent with athletes. — [Raconteur](https://www.raconteur.net/influencer-success/culture-of-success-how-a-yogurt-brand-met-its-influencer-match); search summary citing these campaigns

### Inferences
- The clearest "innovation" in froyo marketing right now is less about new channels and more about compressing the gap between flavor drop and pop-culture relevance — tying LTOs tightly to concurrent entertainment/music news cycles (JVKE's single release window, a film anniversary) so the flavor itself becomes shareable content.
- Small independent shops (The Bigg Chill, Mikono, Kouklas) are winning attention disproportionate to their size through consistent short-form video and UGC reposting, suggesting content cadence now matters as much as media spend for this category on social platforms.

## Is there notable retail/grocery/CPG innovation in frozen yogurt worth noting alongside the restaurant/chain angle?

### Takeaway
The clearest CPG/retail story is the rapid mainstreaming of high-protein, low-sugar frozen dessert pints sold through mass retail (Target, Kroger, Albertsons/Safeway, Sprouts) — led by Magnum/Yasso's first-ever frozen Greek yogurt pint line, upstart Protein Pints' national retail blitz, and protein-brand David Protein's frozen dessert line-extension — all explicitly marketed against "better-for-you"/GLP-1-era demand.

### Cited Findings
- **Yasso** (brand owned by The Magnum Ice Cream Company, which Unilever spun off as an independent public company around November 2025) launched its first pint-format frozen Greek yogurt exclusively at Target on October 12, 2025 (national rollout to follow), extending a product line that previously consisted of bars, "poppables," and sandwiches. Context: Magnum's other brand Ben & Jerry's previously sold a frozen yogurt line but discontinued it. — [Food Dive](https://www.fooddive.com/news/magnum-taps-into-better-for-you-craze-with-yasso-frozen-greek-yogurt-pints/802352/)
- **Protein Pints** (Grand Rapids, MI, founded 2023): grew from 14 Michigan stores in 2024 to roughly 8,000 U.S. retail doors by end of May 2025; added H-E-B, Fresh Thyme Market, Woodman's Markets regionally, then went nationwide in all Kroger-owned stores (March 2025), Target and Albertsons/Safeway (May 2025), and was expanding into all Sprouts Farmers Market locations after a successful pilot. Five (later seven, with two new flavors) SKUs at 30g protein/pint, marketed as 85% less sugar than standard ice cream. — [Refrigerated & Frozen Food](https://www.refrigeratedfrozenfood.com/articles/103586-protein-pints-expands-nationwide-with-target); [Sweets & Snacks World](https://www.sweetsandsnacksworld.com/news/29420/protein-pints-expands-nationally-amid-rising-demand-for-high-protein-ice-cream/); [DairyReporter](https://www.dairyreporter.com/Article/2025/08/05/how-protein-pints-are-pioneering-high-protein-ice-cream/); [Athletech News](https://athletechnews.com/high-protein-ice-cream-brand-hits-major-retailers/)
- **David Protein** (DTC protein-bar brand) entered frozen dessert with a pint line that sold out online in 28 minutes at its June 2026 debut, then launched nationwide at Target in late July 2026 at $7.99/pint (30g protein, 210-260 calories, ≤2g sugar); later added a seasonal "Pumpkin Spice Gold Bars" SKU, following the DTC-to-mass-retail playbook common among protein/functional-food brands. — [Dairy Foods](https://www.dairyfoods.com/articles/99176-david-protein-enters-frozen-dessert-category); [Stack3d](https://www.stack3d.com/2026/07/target-selling-david-protein-ice-cream/)
